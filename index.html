HOW CONNECT 4D WORKS // COMPLETE WEBSITE & TESSERACT GUIDE

By Lorenzo Granado 2026 • https://github.com/axiomgrid

1. The 4 Coordinates (X, Y, Z, W)

Connect 4D is played inside a 4×4×4×4 Tesseract containing 256 cells. Every cell has a unique 4-coordinate address (X, Y, Z, W) where each axis runs from 1 to 4:

  • X (1..4): Left-to-Right column inside a micro-grid or 3D cube.
  • Y (1..4): Bottom-to-Top vertical height on a peg/rod (Gravity axis).
  • Z (1..4): Front-to-Back depth slice (Macro-Row in the 2D Grid).
  • W (1..4): 4th-Dimension Hyper-Cube index (Macro-Column in the 2D Grid).

2. What Makes a 4-in-a-Row in 4D? (520 Winning Lines)

A winning line is any sequence of 4 cells where each coordinate (X, Y, Z, W) steps by +1, -1, or 0 uniformly across 4 consecutive steps (40 canonical direction vectors in total):

1D Orthogonal: 256
1 axis changes (X, Y, Z, or W)
2D Planar Diag: 192
2 axes change simultaneously
3D Cube Diag: 64
3 axes change simultaneously
4D Hyper-Diag: 8
All 4 axes (X,Y,Z,W) step together!

3. The 2D Hyper-Matrix Grid (Left Panel)

The 2D Matrix flattens all 256 cells into a 4×4 Macro-Grid of 4×4 Micro-Grids so you can see the entire game state without any occlusion:

  • • Macro-Columns (W = 1..4) & Macro-Rows (Z = 1..4): Select which 2D slice (W, Z) you are looking at.
  • • Micro-Columns (X = 1..4) & Micro-Rows (Y = 4..1): Inside each small 4×4 box, X goes left-to-right and Y goes bottom (Y1) to top (Y4).
  • • Hide / Show Toggle: Click Hide Grid (or press 1) to collapse the 2D panel and give 100% width to the 3D Hyper-Cubes.

4. Why 10 3D Hyper-Cube Projections? (Right Panel)

Just as a 3D cube can be sliced into 2D squares, a 4D Tesseract can be projected into 10 distinct 3D cubes along the (W, Z) macro-plane so that every single one of the 520 4D winning lines appears as a straight 3D line in at least one cube:

  • • Row 1 (W = 1..4): 4 cubes at fixed W, with Z₁→Z₄ as depth.
  • • Row 2 (Z = 1..4): 4 cubes at fixed Z, with W₁→W₄ as depth.
  • • Row 3 (W = Z & W = -Z): 2 diagonal hyper-slice cubes where W and Z step together (W1Z1..W4Z4 and W1Z4..W4Z1).
  • • Single-Cube Orbit Rotation: Dragging any 3D cube rotates only that individual cube (and the Iso, Top, Front buttons apply to the currently selected cube). Zoom is locked so all cubes stay properly scaled.

5. Header Dashboard, Move Log & PGN

  • • Player 1 & Player 2 / AI Cards: Show whose turn it is, active 3-in-a-row threat count (T), and total placed pieces (P).
  • • State Counter & Move Log: Step backward (← Back) or forward (Forward →) through history, or click any numbered move chip (#0 Start, #1, ...) in the Move Log to jump directly to that board state.
  • • AI Tactical Hint (★ Hint): Highlights the strongest move computed by the heuristic AI engine.
  • • Export / Import PGN & Copy Game Link [Y]: Save or load matches in Connect4D PGN notation, or click Copy Game Link to copy a compressed URL (?g=...) that recreates your exact game state when a friend opens it.
  • • Fullscreen Mode [F]: Hides the header so the 2D and 3D boards fill 100% of the screen.

6. Settings Popup [O]: Physics, AI & Confirmation

  • • Gravity Y-Drop (Default) vs Free 4D Qubic: In Gravity mode, pieces drop to the lowest open vertical level Y = 1..4 in column (X, Z, W). In Free 4D Qubic mode, you can place a piece directly into any of the 256 cells floating in 4D.
  • • 2-Click Confirm (Default) vs 1-Click Instant: In 2-Click mode, your 1st click locks a target cell/column so you can inspect its coordinates and lines; a 2nd click (or Enter) places the piece. In 1-Click mode, clicking places immediately.
  • • Opponent Modes: Play 1P vs AI, 2P Pass & Play, or watch AI vs AI (which plays a random opening move and can be paused/resumed with Space).
  • • AI Difficulty: Choose between Casual, Tactical, and Master (2-Ply Minimax Lookahead).

7. 4D Tactical Assist & Dimension Inspector [X]

  • • Threat Radar [T] (Off by default): Highlights every empty cell that would complete an unblocked 3-in-a-row threat for Player 1 or Player 2.
  • • Ghost Diagonals [L] (Off by default): Projects dashed rays across both 2D and 3D views for every winning line passing through your hovered/locked ghost piece.
  • • Distinct Line IDs [C]: Color-codes and labels each active ghost line (L1..L15) so you can see which 3D cube slices share the exact same 4D line.
  • • 1× Canonical Only [K]: Deduplicates ghost lines so each 4D line is drawn in only one canonical 3D cube.
  • • Inspector Popup [X]: Displays the exact (X, Y, Z, W) coordinates of your locked cell and the complete Diagonal ID Key list.

8. Aesthetics, Themes & Audio Synthesis

  • • Paper & Pencil Aesthetic (Default): Styled like a classic tabletop graph-paper notebook with fountain-pen blue circles (O) for Player 1 and red-pencil hatched crosses (X) for Player 2.
  • • Sci-Fi Matrix Aesthetic: Futuristic hologram interface with glowing 3D spheres and laser beams.
  • • Light & Dark Modes: Switch between warm daylight parchment / lab light mode and dark charcoal / deep-space mode inside Settings (B).
  • • Web Audio Synthesizer [S]: Generates real-time acoustic pencil/wood taps in Paper mode and synthesizer tones in Sci-Fi mode.
